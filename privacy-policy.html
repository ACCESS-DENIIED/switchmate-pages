SwitchMate UK — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 August 2026

SwitchMate UK ("the App") is a free information and tracking tool for UK current account switching. This policy explains what data we do and do not collect.

1. What we collect

  • The App is a directory and tracker. We do not collect, store, or transmit any banking credentials, account numbers, sort codes, or login details from you.
  • The App fetches a public list of current bank-switch offers (a JSON file of publicly available deals) from a remote source. That request may be subject to standard device/network metadata (IP address, device identifiers) as part of normal internet requests, processed by the hosting provider.
  • All switch progress you enter (which offers you're tracking) is stored locally on your own device only. We cannot see it and it is never uploaded.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs in the current version.

2. Third-party links

  • The App contains links to external banking websites (official offer pages), Direct Debit providers, and charities. Once you leave the App, their own privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for their practices.
  • Direct Debits set up through the App's Direct Debit hub are managed by our payment processor (GoCardless) — their privacy policy governs that interaction, not ours.

3. Affiliate links

Some bank links in the App are affiliate links. If you open an account through one, we may receive a small commission at no extra cost to you. This is disclosed in the App. We do not receive any personal data about you from the affiliate network beyond standard click/attribution data.

4. Your data, your control

To delete all App data, clear the App's local storage or uninstall the App. Because nothing is stored on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete.

5. Not financial advice

The App is for educational and informational purposes only. It is not financial advice and we are not an FCA-regulated financial advisor. Bank switch offers and terms change without notice. Always verify on the official bank website before initiating a switch.
